How to play

  1. Every player gets a 5x5 board with numbers 1-25 - type them in yourself or hit Shuffle for a random board.
  2. Players take turns picking a number to eliminate from play - you have 10 seconds on your turn. Eliminated numbers are marked on every board automatically.
  3. A line = 5 marked cells in a row, column or diagonal. Get 5 lines first and hit BINGO!
  4. If several players finish on the same call, the last one to hit BINGO wins.
  5. Both players press Ready to start a match - and again after it ends, to play the next round.
